## 1. Customer Segments
- **Small businesses / startups** — can't afford traditional agencies, need volume at low cost
- **Mid-size brands / agencies** — existing marketing budgets, want AI pipeline integration
- **Entertainment / media companies** — studios, streamers, VP/previsualization needs
- **Individual creators / influencers** — want to scale content output with AI

**Primary entry point:** Mid-size brands and agencies (best fit for $2k–5k/mo retainer)

## 10. Unfair Advantage (Moat)
1. **ComfyUI + Unreal technical depth** — you build pipelines, not just prompt. Competitors can't replicate this without years of experience.
2. **Warm prospects already in pipeline** — market validation before the company legally exists.
3. **Production credibility** — VP Supervisor on indie films, award-winning shorts, Agent00 VP work. This is the trust signal that closes B2B deals.

## 11. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI tools commoditize, margin compresses | Own the workflow/pipeline IP, not just the output |
| USCIS scrutiny of self-employment | Attorney-structured LLC + legitimate W-2 + clear STEM nexus |
| Client churn / slow sales cycle | Keep contract job running until first 2 retainer clients signed |
| Over-dependence on one platform (Instagram algorithm) | Multi-platform output (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn) from day one |
